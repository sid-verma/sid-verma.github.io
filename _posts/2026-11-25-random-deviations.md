A place for curiosity to wander beyond the usual technical essays.

## Trivia

Gather a few friends and explore ten categories, from history and mythology to
music, literature, brands, sport, and the origins of words. Choose a category,
then a question worth 10 to 50 points. A safety screen separates each question
from its answer so the host controls the reveal.

[Play the trivia game]({{ '/random-deviations/trivia/' | relative_url }}){: .btn .btn--primary }

The game includes editable teams, ranked scores, completed-question tracking,
and a host desk. Progress is saved in this browser; export a backup from the
host desk if you want to move a game to another device. The game works without
an account, and its questions and artwork load with the page.

Use your browser’s Back button to return here. All trivia screens live under
this page, with their own Home and Back controls for moving through the game.
